# Payment succeeded but enrollment doesn't match

When a payment has genuinely gone through - money left the account, or the bank sent a
confirmation SMS - but the dashboard still shows "Not Enrolled," the course doesn't appear
under "My Courses," or the wrong course level (Beginner instead of Intermediate, for
example) was activated, this is an enrollment-activation sync problem, not a payment
problem. Do not tell the customer the payment failed or ask them to pay again. Confirm the
payment reference and which course and level were actually intended, and explain that a
reviewer will correct the enrollment record once the payment is confirmed on the account side.

**Common ways customers describe this:** paid for the course but it still shows as Not Enrolled on the dashboard even though the money left the bank account; payment was successful with a confirmation SMS from the bank but the course still doesn't appear under My Courses; payment went through but was enrolled in the Beginner level course instead of the Intermediate level actually paid for.
