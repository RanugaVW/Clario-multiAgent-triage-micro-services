# Account access issues beyond a simple password reset

Some login problems are not solved by a password reset link: an account temporarily locked
after several failed attempts, a session that shows as already active on another device and
blocks a second sign-in, an OTP that can no longer be received because the phone number on
file has changed, a forgotten signup email with no confirmation email to search for, losing
access after an organization's email domain changed so the old work email no longer exists,
or wanting several accidentally-created accounts merged into one without losing progress.
Each needs a different fix - a lockout clears after a short wait or a manual unlock, a stuck
session can be force-cleared from the account side, a changed phone number needs the OTP
destination updated on file, and a lost signup email or account merge needs identity
confirmation before anything is changed. Do not guess which account or email is correct -
confirm identifying details first, and explain that a reviewer completes anything that
touches account identity (merges, an email-on-file change) rather than doing it automatically.

**Common ways customers describe this:** account got locked after several failed login attempts and now shows Account Temporarily Locked; account shows as already logged in on another device, preventing access to the course from another laptop; changed phone number and can no longer receive the OTP tied to the old number, locking out of login; forgot which email address was used to sign up and can't find any confirmation email to check; can't log in after an organization changed its email domain, since the old work email is no longer active; accidentally created multiple accounts with different email addresses and wants them merged into one with all progress intact.
