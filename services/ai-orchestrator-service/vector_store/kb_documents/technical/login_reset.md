# Password reset and sign-in errors

If a customer cannot sign in, confirm they are using the account email that received the
reset link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be requested again from the sign-in
page. Do not ask customers to share passwords, verification codes, or full account details.

**Common ways customers describe this:** can't log in; locked out of my account; forgot my password; reset link isn't working; reset email never arrived; password won't accept; 2FA code not working; verification code not received; can't get into the LMS at all; my account was suddenly blocked with no explanation; account got blocked right before an assignment deadline; blocked for 'suspicious activity' with no unusual logins on my end.

**Additional common phrasings (from real public reviews of comparable LMS platforms — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare):** never receiving the verification code needed to get back into my account; account got locked or deleted and support never responded.

**More real customer wording (second round — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, Pluralsight reviews):** unable to log into my own account for months despite contacting support; locked out of the platform with no way back in.
