# Instructor or course-content changes mid-course

If an instructor changed mid-course or the teaching style no longer matches what the
student signed up for, this needs a human to weigh the specific complaint against what was
actually promised at enrollment. Acknowledge the concern and explain that a reviewer will
assess whether the change materially affected the course before any refund or accommodation
decision is made.

**Common ways customers describe this:** instructor changed partway through the course and teaching style is different; new instructor isn't explaining things well and I want a refund or to switch batches.

**Additional common phrasings (from real public reviews of comparable LMS platforms — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare):** paid for a course expecting full access, but key material wasn't actually included; course didn't include the material that was advertised; how do I access the course; cannot access the course; can't access the course; when recording is published; how to get the recording; enrolled but can't see the actual course content; only the dashboard.

**More real customer wording (second round — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, Pluralsight reviews):** practical exercises or labs in the course don't actually work.
