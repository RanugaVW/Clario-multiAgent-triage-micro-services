# Currency and exchange rates

Charges are displayed in the currency shown at checkout. Bank exchange rates and foreign
transaction fees are set by the payment provider. For a difference between checkout and a
settled bank charge, collect the transaction date and displayed amount for billing review.

**Common ways customers describe this:** charged in the wrong currency; exchange rate seems off; foreign transaction fee surprise; price different from what I saw at checkout; converted amount looks wrong; bank charged more than shown.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** want to pay in my local currency; asking for multi-currency support; checkout page shows the wrong currency; marketplace prices displayed in the wrong currency; want to change which currency my subscription is billed in.
