# Chargebacks and disputes

If a customer reports an unrecognized charge, ask them to confirm the date, amount, and
merchant description. Explain the available dispute process and route the case promptly to
billing review. Do not ask the customer to share bank credentials or a full card number.

**Common ways customers describe this:** don't recognize this charge; unauthorized charge on my card; want to dispute a charge; filed a chargeback; unknown transaction; someone else used my card; this wasn't me.
