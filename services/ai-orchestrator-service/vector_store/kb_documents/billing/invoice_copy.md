# Invoice and receipt copies

Customers can request an invoice or receipt using the purchase email and transaction date.
Before sending a document, verify the requester can access the account. Redact sensitive
payment data from any internal notes and never send a full card number in a receipt.

**Common ways customers describe this:** need a receipt; invoice never received; can't find my invoice; need proof of payment; please resend the receipt; need an invoice for taxes; need a copy of my invoice; can't find my receipt; need proof of purchase; offer not applied; chrged the full payment.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** invoice total looks wrong; invoice is missing the VAT amount; invoice says paid but the account still shows unpaid; can't update my billing address; want to export my invoice.
