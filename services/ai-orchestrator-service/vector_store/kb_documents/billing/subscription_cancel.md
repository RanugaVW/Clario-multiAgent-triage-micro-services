# Subscription cancellation

Explain the documented cancellation path in account billing settings. Confirm the renewal
date and whether cancellation takes effect immediately or at the end of the current billing
period. Do not state that a refund is guaranteed; eligibility requires billing review.

**Common ways customers describe this:** want to cancel my subscription; how do I cancel; still being charged after cancelling; cancel and get a refund; stop auto-renewal; want to stop my subscription; cancel before renewal; can't find a cancel option anywhere on the dashboard; want written confirmation I won't be charged again; cancelled before but got charged again this month; want to cancel and get a refund since I haven't attended any classes yet; want to cancel but keep my access until the billing period ends; can't cancel because I can't log in to my account; membership auto-renewed for a full year without any warning; renewal reminder email went to spam and I was charged before I noticed; nearly impossible to actually unsubscribe; cancelled during the trial but got charged anyway; support is AI-only, no way to reach a real person to cancel; cancelled the same day I was charged and still got refused a refund; the cancel or auto-renew setting is hidden deep in account settings; only offered a yearly plan, so missing the cancellation window locks you in for a full year; charged for a renewal with absolutely no notice beforehand.
