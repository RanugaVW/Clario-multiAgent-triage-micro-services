# Updating a payment method

Customers can update a payment method through secure billing settings. Support must not
request a complete card number, CVV, bank password, or one-time bank code. If an update fails,
collect the safe error message and route it to billing support.

**Common ways customers describe this:** can't update my card; payment method won't save; updating billing info fails; adding a new card doesn't work; can't remove my old card; card keeps getting rejected.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** add payment method button doesn't work; need more international payment options; want to link more than one payment method to my account.
