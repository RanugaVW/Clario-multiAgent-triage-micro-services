# Plan changes

Tell the customer where to review available plans and the effective date of a change. Before
confirming price differences, refer to the displayed plan details or transaction record.
Escalate unclear prorated charges to billing rather than estimating an amount.

**Common ways customers describe this:** want to upgrade my plan; want to downgrade my subscription; switch to a different plan; price changed after upgrading; confused about a prorated charge; want to switch plans; upgrade/downgrade my subscription; why did my price change; enrolled in the wrong course by mistake and want to switch; discount code didn't apply at checkout; charged full price despite an advertised offer.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** my downgrade isn't applying; can't change my plan from the mobile app; the plan-change page just hangs; mistakenly joined.

**More real customer wording (second round — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, Pluralsight reviews):** wasn't clear if I'd keep access to my materials after cancelling; tried to switch from monthly to yearly billing and it didn't work.
