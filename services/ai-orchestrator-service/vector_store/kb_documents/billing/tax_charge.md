# Tax charges

Tax is calculated from the billing address and applicable local rules. Ask the customer to
review their billing address and invoice. If the displayed tax appears incorrect, send the
invoice date, region, and amount to billing review without making a tax determination.

**Common ways customers describe this:** tax amount looks wrong; extra tax charged; question about VAT; sales tax seems incorrect; why was I taxed on this; why was I charged tax; tax amount looks wrong, tax on my invoice.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** need my tax ID included on the receipt; getting an 'invalid tax calculation' error at checkout; want to export my payment history for tax records.
