# Payment linked to the wrong course, account, or amount

Sometimes a real, successful payment ends up attached to the wrong thing: a bank slip gets
linked to the wrong course, a transfer goes to an old or incorrect account number found from
an outdated source, a typo enters a smaller amount than the actual course fee, or a customer
is accidentally enrolled in two courses when only one was meant to be paid. These are data-
correction issues, not disputes over whether a payment happened - confirm the transaction
details the customer already has (reference, amount, date, which course was intended) and
explain that a reviewer will correct the linkage or adjust the enrollment once verified. Do
not confirm a correction has already been made, or promise a specific refund amount, before
that review happens.

**Common ways customers describe this:** uploaded a bank slip but it appears to have been linked to the wrong course instead of the one actually paid for; transferred money to an old or incorrect bank account number found from an outdated post and now the payment can't be matched; made a WebXpay payment but accidentally entered a smaller amount than the actual course fee due to a typo; accidentally enrolled in both courses when only one was meant to be a paid purchase and wants the extra one refunded.
