# Failed payment with pending funds

An unsuccessful checkout can leave a temporary pending authorization. Ask the customer to
check whether the transaction is pending or settled. A pending authorization is normally
released by the bank; a settled charge should be sent to billing review with the date and
amount, without collecting full payment-card information.

**Common ways customers describe this:** payment declined; payment failed but money was taken; pending charge won't go away; transaction failed; card declined but I was still charged; payment didn't go through; checkout failed but money still on hold; transaction declined.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** payment failed and now my account is suspended; payment error happening specifically on the mobile app; payment failed but the charge still went through.
