# Refund eligibility and partial refunds

Questions about *whether* a refund is allowed - how many days after enrollment a request
still qualifies, whether completing part of a course still counts as eligible, or whether
persistent technical issues that blocked access justify a refund - are eligibility
questions, not refund-status questions, and the exact eligibility window or partial-refund
calculation is not something to state from memory. Confirm the enrollment date and how much
of the course was actually accessed, and explain that a reviewer will check the request
against the current eligibility policy before approving or denying it. Do not state a
specific day-count window or refund percentage unless it is already confirmed in the
customer's own account record.

**Common ways customers describe this:** completed only the first week of the course and is asking for a partial refund instead of continuing; asking how many days after enrollment a refund can still be requested before losing eligibility; wants a refund because persistent technical issues prevented accessing most of the course content.
