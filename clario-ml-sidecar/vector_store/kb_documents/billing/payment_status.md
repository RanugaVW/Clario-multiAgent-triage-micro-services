# Manual payment proof still pending or unmatched

A bank slip, cash-deposit receipt, or bank-transfer reference can sit in "Pending
Verification" for a few business days before a human confirms it - this is normal, not a
sign the payment was lost, but a wait beyond a few days is worth escalating rather than
asking the customer to keep waiting indefinitely. The same manual review applies when the
name on a slip doesn't match the registered student (for example, a family member's account
was used to pay), when the original slip has been lost and the customer needs another way to
prove payment, when a payment was made under a corporate sponsorship arrangement and needs
linking to the sponsoring company's record, or when bank or transfer fees were deducted so
the amount received looks short of the full course fee. None of these can be resolved by
policy citation alone - ask for the same identifying details already used elsewhere
(transaction reference, approximate amount, date) without requesting full card numbers or
bank credentials, and explain that a reviewer will manually confirm the payment.

**Common ways customers describe this:** uploaded a bank deposit slip for the course three days ago but it still shows Pending Verification; paid using a family member's bank account so the name on the slip doesn't match the registered name and worries it won't be accepted; lost the physical bank slip after making a cash deposit and is asking how else to prove payment; made a bank transfer with a reference number but it hasn't been linked to the student account after several days; submitted a bank slip as part of a corporate sponsorship arrangement and wants confirmation it's linked correctly to the sponsoring company; bank charges or transfer fees were deducted from the payment so the amount received was short of the full course fee.
