# Duplicate charges

Ask the customer for the dates and amounts of the duplicate charges, without requesting full
card numbers or bank credentials. Verify whether one entry is a pending authorization. If two
settled charges exist for one purchase, open a billing review and explain that the team will
confirm the transaction history before a refund is issued.

**Common ways customers describe this:** charged twice; double charged; two charges for one purchase; billed multiple times; duplicate transaction; charged again by mistake; two charges for one order; double billed; charged twice for the same course; double charge showing on my bank statement; charged for two courses when I only meant to pay for one.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** charged twice but the account still shows as unpaid; double charged for the same subscription two months in a row.
