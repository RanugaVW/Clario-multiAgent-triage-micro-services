# Refund status

Confirm the original purchase date and the refund confirmation date. Refunds return to the
original payment method and can take several business days to appear, depending on the bank.
Do not promise an exact settlement date unless it is present in the transaction record.

**Common ways customers describe this:** where is my refund; refund never arrived; how long does a refund take; refund shows as pending; money isn't back yet; refund hasn't shown up yet; how long do refunds take; refund hasn't arrived even though I was told it would be processed; refund request was denied with no explanation; want to know exactly why my refund was denied.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** asking for a refund because of poor course quality; want a refund because the course never actually started; requesting a refund after the app kept crashing on me.

**Additional common phrasings (from real public reviews of comparable LMS platforms — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare):** customer support won't respond to my refund request; refund refused even though the course content didn't match what was advertised; told the refund window had already passed; refund denied under a strict 14-day policy.

**More real customer wording (second round — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, Pluralsight reviews):** refund refused because I'd already watched a couple of the introductory videos; asked for a refund after barely using the course and was told no; refund request denied with no real explanation given.
