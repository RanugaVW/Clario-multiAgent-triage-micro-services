# Refunds requiring human judgment

Medical emergencies, parental-consent issues, and relocation partway through a course each
require reviewing evidence or circumstances a policy document cannot cover in advance.
Acknowledge the situation, explain that a human reviewer will look at the specific
circumstances, and set an expectation for when they will hear back. Do not promise a refund
amount, approval, or timeline before that review has happened.

**Common ways customers describe this:** needs a refund due to a sudden medical emergency that prevents continuing the course; a parent requesting a refund because their minor child was enrolled without proper parental consent; needs a refund because relocating abroad partway through the course and won't be able to attend the remaining live sessions; unsure whether still within the free-trial refund window or already past it and wants clarity before formally requesting a refund.
