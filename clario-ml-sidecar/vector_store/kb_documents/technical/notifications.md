# Missing notifications

Check that notifications are enabled in both the product settings and device settings. Ask
the customer to update the app and test a new notification. Escalate with device model,
operating-system version, app version, and the time of the missed notification if unresolved.

**Common ways customers describe this:** not getting notifications; alerts aren't showing up; push notifications missing; notification sound not playing; no email alerts; notifications arriving late; not getting notified, alerts stopped coming through; missed a reminder; push notifications not showing up; not getting notified; missed a reminder; push notifications not showing up.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** notification badge won't clear; notification settings keep resetting on their own; getting a suspension notification that doesn't match my actual account status.
