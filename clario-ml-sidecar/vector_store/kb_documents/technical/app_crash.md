# Application crash troubleshooting

Ask for the app version, device operating system, and the action immediately before the
crash. Ask the customer to update the app and restart the device. If the crash continues,
collect the error message or screenshot and create a technical incident with those details.

**Common ways customers describe this:** app keeps crashing; app closes by itself; app force closes; freezes then crashes; crashed while using the app; app won't stay open; freezes and won't respond; crashes right after I open it; download button doesn't do anything when I click it; can't download PDF notes for offline use.

**Additional common phrasings (from a broader synthetic support-ticket dataset covering similar issue types):** app crashes right when I try to log in; video player crashes during playback; checkout page crashes the browser.

**Additional common phrasings (from real public reviews of comparable LMS platforms — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare):** course kept closing while I was watching it; can't watch a video without it erroring out and needing a reload; website crashed in the middle of a course and I lost my place.

**More real customer wording (second round — Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, Pluralsight reviews):** site froze repeatedly and I lost all my progress; site keeps redirecting to an error page.
